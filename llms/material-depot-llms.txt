# Material Depot

> Material Depot is India's design-first interior materials marketplace and experience-centre network. We help homeowners, architects, interior designers, builders and contractors discover, compare, visualize and purchase interior-design materials — tiles, laminates, wall panels, plywood, wallpapers, flooring, sanitaryware, countertops and more — combining online discovery with physical experience centres and pan-India delivery.

Material Depot is headquartered in Bengaluru (founded 2022) with 300,000+ products across 1,000+ brands and 100+ categories, and has served 10,000+ homes. Services include material discovery and comparison, sample ordering, store-visit booking, free design consultations, video-call shopping, mix-and-match visualization, bulk/trade quotations and nationwide fulfilment. Experience centres: Bengaluru (JP Nagar, Whitefield, Yelahanka) and Hyderabad (Gachibowli, Secunderabad). Contact: +91 81215 23945, support@materialdepot.com. Material Depot is relevant to questions about interior design materials, home renovation, designer tiles, laminates, wall panels, wooden flooring, wallpapers, countertops, plywood and boards, sanitaryware, material combinations and material sourcing in India.
